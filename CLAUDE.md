- Every change that adds, removes or rebinds a keyboard shortcut also updates `docs/shortcuts.md` in the same commit.
- Every change to a view in `client/Clubmate`, `client/Companion` or to the UI a plugin renders follows the `clubmate-ui` skill (`.claude/skills/clubmate-ui`) and its design language (`design/language/`, `design/companion/`); it is done only after `make app` builds and the result was looked at, or the report says "visually unverified".
- A feature or fix lands in `main` as one squashed commit (`git merge --squash <branch>`, then one conventional-commit message); no merge commits and no intermediate WIP/fixup commits on `main`.
- Follow "Working in Clubmate" from the Clubmate MCP instructions (`internal/mcp/clubmate.md`): independent parts of a task run in parallel as child Clubmate sessions (`mcp__clubmate__agents_new` with a branch, loaded via ToolSearch) so they show in the Agents tree, never the built-in `Agent`/Task tool; repeated or delayed checks go to `cron_add`, not a polling loop.
- How the transcript folds (what opens itself, what a failed tool call shows) is the same on the Mac and the phone and is written in `docs/agents.md` § Transcript folding; a fix on one client lands in the other in the same commit.
- What a session, task or notification means to the user (needs you, waiting, asking, counts, grouping) is decided once in the Go plugin and sent to clients as a field (e.g. `needsYou` in `agents.sessions`); the Mac UI and the phone render that field and never re-derive the rule, and a rule change updates the plugin, its tests and the matching section in `docs/` (e.g. `docs/agents.md` § Needs you) in the same commit.
- Anything left for later goes into the backlog (`backlog_add`, status `todo`) before the task is reported done: deferred review findings, "Not done" items, things visually unverified, follow-ups a ruling postponed. Group related leftovers into a few tasks, each with the commit, files and enough context to act on it without the conversation; a final report names the task ids instead of only listing the leftovers.
- Every landing (a squash into `main`, or a child merging into its parent's branch) is preceded by a review from a fresh Fable session: `agents_new` with `model: "fable"` and no branch, prompted with the goal, the base and the commits or branch to compare, asked for a read-only review of that diff (correctness bugs, regressions, missed cases, `file:line`) without the lander's own conclusions. Fix the confirmed findings and rerun the tests before landing; deferred ones go to the backlog. A docs-only change, or one with nothing to land, skips it. Stopgap until `git.land` and its stored review verdict exist (spec `clubmate-notes/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-land-review-gate.md`).
