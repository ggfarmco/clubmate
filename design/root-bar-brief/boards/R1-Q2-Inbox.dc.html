Does the count match "Needs you"?

There's no count in the bar, so it can't drift. The mark, the menu's "n waiting", the VoiceOver value and the "Needs you" chip all read the same plugin field, needsYou. Unread Inbox rows can turn the glyph full on their own, but they never add to the "needs you" number.
