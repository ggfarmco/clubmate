Mic: top or bottom?

Final ruling: bottom, B2. On tab roots it's a voice bar in the system's bottom-accessory slot above the tab bar. It sits in thumb reach, keeps press-and-hold, shows the live waveform, and folds inline when the tab bar minimizes. Pushed pages without a composer (graph run, open queue) mount the same voice bar in a bottom safe-area inset, and the floating mic (B4) retires. In a session the composer mic is unchanged. The top bar becomes [Inbox] · [New]. B3, the native top mic, is the fallback.

Facts this rests on

  • The tab bar hides on every pushed page and returns on pop, so a bottom mic costs no nav-bar hosting. It pops in with the tab bar at the end of a back swipe (#1121), not on its own.
  • Only a hosted view can keep hold-to-talk; the bottom accessory is designed to host one.
  • The composer mic is already at the bottom inside a session.
  • tabBarMinimizeBehavior(.onScrollDown): B1 and B2 must survive the minimized bar.

B1 · not taken Circle at the tab bar's end

  • Tab(role: .search) is a tab, not a button. Selecting it switches the content and morphs the tab bar into a search field. VoiceOver calls it a tab. It takes no press-and-hold, and it uses up the search slot.
  • "Same look" means our own circle overlaid beside the system bar. We'd have to keep it in sync with the bar's layout and its minimize animation ourselves, and it's still a hosted view, just without a system slot to live in.
  • Only win: it stays a circle when minimized. B2 does the same, with a system slot.

B2 · ruled Bottom accessory

  • Idle: mic + "Talk to shop" (the workspace name) + caption. Press and hold talks and release sends. Tap goes hands-free.
  • Listening: Cancel · live accent waveform · timer · accent Send. Green only means live; there's no red.
  • Minimized: the system folds it inline beside the tab button and hides the caption. It's still one target, still in thumb reach.
  • It hides with the tab bar on every push. On pushed pages without a composer the same view comes back as a bottom inset. In a session the composer mic takes over in the same place, with the same gestures.
  • The cost: about 56 pt of height on every root while the bar is expanded.

B3 · fallback Top bar mic, tap only

  • Fully native and costs 0 ms, but out of thumb reach, and hold-to-talk on roots is gone.
  • Use it if B2's accessory still exceeds the pop budget after the trim below, or if the accessory doesn't render on iPad.

B4 · not taken Floating mic on roots

  • It works on graph run and open queue because nothing else is at the bottom there. On a root it has to clear the tab bar, and when the bar minimizes, the tab circle lands in the same leading corner. So B4 floats about 96 pt up, over the rows' leading status glyphs.
  • That needs a hand-made ~62 pt content margin that tracks the minimize state, and it stacks two floating layers on the left.
  • Its one real edge: as root content it slides in with the page on a back swipe instead of popping in with the tab bar. Fixing #1121 closes that gap for B2.

Pushed pages without a composer

The accessory belongs to the TabView and hides with the tab bar, so on graph run and open queue it's gone. Those pages mount the same VoiceAccessory view themselves in a bottom safe-area inset: a full-width glass capsule where the tab bar would be. The system reserves its height, so the last row is never covered.

The floating 50 pt circle retires. The result is one mic look outside sessions and the composer mic inside them, all at the bottom, all hold + tap. If the voice panel shows the live transcript, it opens above the capsule the way it opens above the circle today.
