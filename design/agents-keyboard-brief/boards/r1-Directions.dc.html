Three ways to move between the tree, the transcript and sessions

The ask: get between the sessions list and the transcript from the keyboard, and switch sessions fast. All three directions share one session layer — ⌃⌘ means “sessions”, as ⌃⌘↓ ↑ already does — and treat the transcript as a tree of blocks moved with the tree's own keys. They differ in how focus moves, what ⎋ does, and what a number means.

A

Ring

One key walks the panes, the ⌃⌘ layer walks the sessions. Nothing you know changes; ⎋ still stops.

⎋ in the composer

Stays: it stops the turn. Leaving the composer is ⌃⇥. The iPad keeps “⎋ never stops”, so the two models stay apart on this one key.

How the keys show

  • Focus mark: the 2 pt bar on the focused tree row or transcript block, the green ring on the composer. One accent mark at a time.
  • Hold ⌃ for ½ s: digits 1–9 replace the detail on the first nine rows.
  • Status bar: the focused pane's keys, e.g. ⌃⇥ pane · j/k step · l/h fold.
  • Sheet: ⌘/ from anywhere.

B

Modes

⎋ leaves typing, bare keys drive the rest — the vim grammar the tree already has, carried into the transcript. ⎋ means the same on Mac and iPad.

⎋ in the composer

Changes: ⎋ steps out — composer → transcript → tree → composer. ⌘. stops the turn from any pane, on both platforms. A running turn left alone is safe; an accidental stop is not.

How the keys show

  • Focus mark as in A, plus the pane name first in the status bar: TREE READ TYPE.
  • After g: digits on the rows and a which-key line in the status bar (g → 1–7 session · l last · n needs you).
  • A focused card prints bare 1 2 3 instead of ⌥1 ⌥2 ⌥3.
  • Sheet: ? in tree and transcript, ⌘/ anywhere.

C

Switcher

Sessions behave like apps: ⌃⇥ flips to the last one, hold it for the list. ⌥⌘ arrows move between panes by direction.

⎋ in the composer

Stays: it stops the turn, and ⌘. stops from any pane. Leaving the composer is ⌥⌘← or ⌥⌘↑.

How the keys show

  • Focus mark as in A; the titles of panes without focus dim to muted.
  • Pinned rows print their slot digit always — only pinned rows, so few.
  • The switcher lists sessions most recent first, needs-you icons kept, pin digits printed. No digit picks inside it: ⌃+digit is a Spaces key.
  • Sheet: ⌘/.

For

  • Fastest toggle: one chord, one hand.
  • Numbers never move: pins are yours.
  • Arrow directions need no learning.

Against

  • Pins are set-up work and go stale.
  • A new surface (the switcher).
  • ⌥⌘ + arrows is a stretch on a laptop.
  • ⌃⇥ loses its macOS meaning.
