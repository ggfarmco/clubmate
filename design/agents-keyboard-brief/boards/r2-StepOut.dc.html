Stepping out without ending full screen

On the Mac an ⎋ that no view takes travels up to the window, and AppKit leaves full screen. B makes ⎋ the step-out key, so in full screen one ⎋ too many would throw the window out. The fix has two parts: the Agents section takes every ⎋ itself, so none reaches the window; and there is a way to step out that is not ⎋ at all.

Part 1 · Agents takes every ⎋

Rule. Every Agents pane — tree, transcript, composer, cards, aside, the tree drawer — handles ⎋. Where there is nothing to step out of, ⎋ is still consumed and the status bar says so. No ⎋ pressed inside Agents reaches the window, on the Mac or the iPad.

Leaving full screen stays where macOS puts it: ⌃⌘F, Globe F, or the green button. ⌃⌘F is free in the app. (On the iPad ⌃⌘F is the reading view; there is no full screen to lose.)

Outside Agents. Other rail sections keep today's behaviour; the same rule is worth taking app-wide later.

Decision

⎋ stays the ladder key (habit, and it is the iPad's key too), consumed everywhere. ⌃[ stays its alias. New: ⌥⌘← ⌥⌘↑ ⌥⌘↓ ⌥⌘→ go straight to a pane — tree, transcript, composer, aside — from anywhere in Agents, the composer included. They never touch the window, and the prompt → tree hop becomes one chord instead of ⎋ ⎋.

⌥⌘ arrows were C's pane keys in r1; they join B as a second way, not a replacement for the ladder.
