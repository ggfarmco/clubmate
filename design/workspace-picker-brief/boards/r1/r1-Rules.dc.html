Workspace picker · round 1 · rules

A, B, C compared — pick C

C (Mac strip sheet) fixes everything the brief lists, stays one list long with 3 Macs × 10, costs M, and uses the same structure as the iPad sidebar. A is the fallback if we won't build a custom sheet yet; everything it needs carries over to C.

Why in the workspace list: the picker shows 30 workspaces at once and should not load 30 session lists; what counts as running, waiting or failed is decided once in Go; and idle time has no source today. Mac totals are plain sums on the phone; they add no meaning.

6 · Why C

  • Fixes each problem in the brief. Plain menu → one list with status per row; same folder glyph → shape is status; Mac Details among workspaces → beside the strip; grey Mac header → coloured chip plus a state line; no activity → waiting, running, failed and last-active per row; cut-off Pair a Mac → leading button in the sheet.
  • Scales by Mac, not by the total. 3 Macs × 10 shows 11 rows that fit on screen; no fold rule, so rows never jump.
  • Costs M, not L. One list, one strip, and the session row's status circle from C3.
  • Same shape on iPad. The sidebar is "Mac → its workspaces" too; the popover is the same view.
  • Trade-off we accept: a wait on another Mac is a count on a chip, not a row. Answering across Macs stays the Inbox's job.
  • If the custom sheet must wait: ship A first (S). Its glyph rules, strings and the activity field carry over to C unchanged.
