# Companion design brief

Input for a Claude Design pass over the iPhone/iPad companion: upload `screens/*.png` with the Mac boards named in `prompt.md`, paste `prompt.md`.

## Re-capturing the screens

1. `make companion`; `xcrun simctl create cm-brief "iPhone 18 Pro"`, boot it, `xcrun simctl status_bar <udid> override --time 9:41`.
2. Per screen: `xcrun simctl ui <udid> appearance dark|light`, install `build/CompanionDerivedData/Build/Products/Debug-iphonesimulator/Companion.app`, launch with `-fixtures client/CompanionKit/Tests/CompanionKitTests/Fixtures -autoConfirmPairing YES -pair 'clubmate://pair?v=1&c=ABCDEF&h=100.101.1.2&p=47622&id=mac-mock&n=Mac%20Studio&e=4000000000' -open <sidebar|agents|agents/<session>|inbox>`, wait ~7 s, `xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot <file>`.
   `-open` picks a tab of the root and pushes one screen on its stack: `<plugin>[/<detail>[/<item>]]` (`agents`, `backlog`, `more`, e.g. `backlog/9`) switches to that tab and pushes the detail; `inbox` pushes Inbox on Agents; `docs` opens Agents and `docs/<path>` the doc over it; `tab:<agents|backlog|wiki|more|search>` switches the tab and keeps its stack (wiki and search show only once the Mac offers them, T10/T12); `push:<session/<id>|run/<id>|task/<n>|doc/<path>|section/<id>|inbox|mac>` pushes that destination on its own tab (Agents for a doc or Inbox); `pair` opens Add Mac; `sidebar` stays on the first tab. With `-restoreStacks YES` the saved stacks come back and are saved again.
3. Three Macs like the C5 boards (Mac Studio blue, MacBook Pro magenta, MacBook Air graphite that goes offline after 3 s): add `-macs 3`. `-open mac` opens the first Mac's details (colour picker).
   `-open report` opens the Report a Problem sheet for the first Mac over the Macs screen (with `-offline <seconds>` its Send queues; the fixture Mac says `reports` in `hello` and answers `report` with `r-fixture`);
   `-open mac` shows the Keep Awake group (`awake.state`, the one-switch shape of `docs/awake.md`): off by default; `-awake armed` chooses While agents work on a laptop on battery (armed, nothing holds), `-awake hours` 4 hours plus an agent's "release build" hold that opens the first session, `-awake on` Until turned off; the Keep awake picker answers through `awake.set {mode, hours}` and publishes `awake.changed`. With `-offline <seconds>` the group stays dimmed as "Asleep or offline".
   `-usage normal|warning|reached` makes the fixture Macs answer `agents.usage` (the subscription windows, `design/features/limit-share/`): Mac Studio and MacBook Air on alex@acme.example (5h 42%, 91% warning or 100% reached; 7d 81%), MacBook Pro on alex@home.example (12%, 30%), and gives `agt_0000000000000001` and `agt_0000000000000004` shares (≈6% / ≈3%, ≈2% / <1%). `-usage reached` also adds two sessions the limit stopped (`agt_0000000000000031`, `agt_0000000000000032`) under "Paused until ‹now + 2 h›" (C-UL-A-Reached). `-open agents -usage normal` shows the header line; add `-macs 3` for the account label and "Your other Macs" in Claude usage (tap the line, or launch with `-open usage` to open the sheet over the first workspace); ••• → Session Info on `agt_0000000000000001` shows its shares. On an iPad the line sits at the top of the sessions column, as on the phone.
   `-open connection` opens the first Mac's Connection sheet over its details; `-checkFixture pass|fail` makes Run check use a fake network probe (pass: a Tailscale address and every port open; fail: no Tailscale address and every port silent), since the simulator can't reach the fixture Mac's addresses.
   `-open more` shows the More segment, `-open more/<cron|processes|triggers>` a plugin section from `Fixtures/sdui.json`, `-open more/<section>/<item id>` its main view as if that sidebar row was tapped (selects the row on a real Mac; the fixture's main tree is static); `-empty cron.main` swaps that slot for its tree under `empty` in `Fixtures/sdui.json` (Cron without jobs); `-open agents/<session> -docOpened YES` publishes `docs.opened` for the first session ~3 s after launch (the "Opened …" banner on its screen); add `-noUIBridge YES` to act like a Mac without the UI bridge (`hello.ui` false: no More).
   `-open agents -search <query>` types the query into the Agents search field (the fixture's `agents.search` matches titles and fixture transcript text); `-open backlog -search <query>` into the Backlog one (`4` pins #4, whose fixture tags are `checkout, refactor`; `999` reads "No task #999 · last is #11").
   `-holdFixture <seconds>` (`YES` means 4) changes the first workspace's list that long after its first `agents.sessions`: "Add stock reservation expiry" turns to wanting `git fetch` and "Nightly: deps audit" starts in main, so a list on screen holds them under the "1 needs you · 1 new" pill (session order, `design/features/session-order`, C phone boards). The fixture's sessions sit in two worktrees, main and `chore/cart-store`.
   `-runs YES` adds two graph runs that need you to Mac Studio's `clubmate` workspace (`gr_0000000000000004` waits for a step's approval, `gr_0000000000000001` is past a round cap; `graphs.show/approve/continue/stop` answer them): `-workspace prj_00000000000000f2 -open agents` for the list (C-Step-NeedsYou), `-open agents/gr_0000000000000004` for the run screen (C-Step-Approve). `-runAnsweredAfter <seconds>` answers the step run "on the Mac" that long after launch (the card drops, the header turns Running).
   `-openFixture YES` adds done sessions with open items to Mac Studio's `shop` workspace, the fixture's `agents.open-*` commands answer them: `agt_0000000000000021` (2 decisions, 3 offers; C-Q2-Session), `agt_0000000000000022` (1 decision, 1 offer), and `agt_0000000000000023` with `mayAsk` ("may have left a question", Turn into Items in •••). `-open agents` for the list (C-Q2-Open-Light), `-open agents/open` for the queue (C-Q2-Queue). Add `-openStress YES` for `agt_0000000000000024`: a long title, 3 decisions + 4 offers from two turns, numbered by the fixture like the plugin, and "+2 more went to the backlog". Each session with items also has a hidden `agents_open_items` call that must draw no row.
   `-landReview YES` adds land-gate landings to Mac Studio's `shop` workspace (`design/features/land-review`, R2-C-Phone): `agt_00000000000000b1` under review whose Round 1 reviewer (`…b2`) asks to run a command and nests under it in Needs you, `…b3` with "3 changes" (its reviewer `…b4` hidden), `…b5` in "review 2/2…" (reviewers `…b6` running and `…b7` closed, both hidden) `…b8` "approved", `…b9` held by a Done gate ("Held · plans and specs · Plan pipeline…") and `…ba` with a Done gate's rounds-used card (One more round, Accept as done, Stop; C-GT-A-Card), and Tool call gates (C-GT-A-Held-Light, ruling 13): `…bb` with a call held by one gate (open `agents/agt_00000000000000bb` for the block), `…bc` held by two gates ("Held · git push +1 · 2 graphs reviewing") and `…bd` with a refused call, the gate's message under it and a no-turn refusal (their Open review opens the hidden reviewer `…be`); the list answers with the Land gates dock row and the fixture serves `agents.gates` (`Fixtures/sdui.json` under `landReview`, built by the plugin's own view code), whose Open review and Open landing answer `{open: {session}}`. Allowing `…b2`'s command hides it again.
   `-wiki YES` gives every workspace a Wiki section (the Wiki tab shows): a tree `pages` with Shop wiki, Checkout flow and notes › Retry notes; a `select` on it answers with that page in `wiki.main` (a header and a long `document`); `wiki.locate` answers `{id, path, title}` for those pages and for `/Users/me/src/shop/docs/checkout-retries.md` (so the session link "the retries plan" opens a wiki page), `{}` for any other path. Shop wiki links to Checkout flow at its top and its foot, and to `docs/payments.md`, a doc. Add `-wikiOnStudio YES` with `-macs 3` to keep the Wiki section on Mac Studio only, so the tab set changes when you go to a workspace of the other Macs.
   `-taskFixture connect|connectMany|workspace|land|failed|archive|afk` replaces the first workspace's sessions with a multi-repo task (`agt_0000000000000031`, the `C-MR-*` boards; the workspace reads "ads" with 2 folders). A queued action stays in the app's `queue.json` across launches and goes out on the next one, so erase the simulator or expect it to settle the fixture.
   `-failureAction signin|continue` changes the failed fixture session's (`agt_0000000000000005`) action from Retry to the Mac-only Sign in line or Hand off. `-reopenAction resume` gives the closed session (`agt_0000000000000003`) Reopen (`agents.resume`) instead of Hand off.
   `-questionFixture YES` makes `agt_0000000000000004` ask two questions (the paged question card); add `-questionSingle YES` for one single-choice question, where a tap answers at once. Add `-questionLong YES` for the stress case: five options with long descriptions and a long question (with `-questionSingle`, the agent's message is a long paragraph).

   `-asideFixture YES` turns on the question fixture and gives `agt_0000000000000004` one aside (`agt_00000000000000b1`, on option 2 of question 1). `-asideFixture many` adds a passage aside, so the session header shows the "2 asides" pill. `-asideFixture empty` only lets the session have asides. The fake `agents.aside*` commands prefill the card, set the composer draft, keep the aside or close it (`C-SA-A-*` boards, `client/CompanionUITests/AsideUITests.swift`).
   `-longTranscript YES` gives `agt_0000000000000004` 121 rows of very different heights (some 60 lines long, ending in "Last reply of the long session"), the case where a lazy transcript opened away from its last row.
   `-manyImages YES` gives `agt_0000000000000004` 60 messages with one image each (`shot-1.png` … `shot-60.png`), more than the phone keeps in memory (48), so tiles must load when scrolled into view and again after eviction.
   `-doc <path>` opens the docs sheet on the first workspace; the fixture serves `/Users/me/src/shop/docs/checkout-retries.md`, `payments.md`, the diagram `checkout-flow.mmd` and the artifact `conversion.html` (a Chart.js bar chart from jsdelivr with a button that hides a series; from `Fixtures/docs-read.json`), any other path fails with `not in workspace`, and each doc changes once 4 s after its first read.
   `-staleEdit YES` refuses every `backlog.update` as `stale` and renames that task on the "Mac" first (Edit stays open with "Changed on the Mac" and the Mac's title; after Cancel, the refused line with the typed text, what it changed and Send again, the list showing the Mac's title); the fixture's `backlog.list {since}` answers by cursor (`fx.<n>`), deltas included, and refuses a write whose `seen` is older than that task's last change. `-renameAfter <seconds>` renames #9 on the "Mac" that long after launch and publishes `backlog.changed` (an edit started before it is refused as changed on the Mac; Save in the still-open Edit, or Send again on the refused line, then lands).
   `-failCommands <id,id…>` makes those commands fail with `context deadline exceeded` while the Mac stays online (a weak link): with `backlog.list,docs.list,agents.sessions` the lists show the copy saved on the phone by an earlier launch, and its "as of" line once that copy is over 10 minutes old.
4. An offline Mac: `-offline <seconds>` makes the fixture Mac unreachable that many seconds after launch (the event stream drops, requests fail); `-offline 0` on a relaunch without `-pair` shows a paired Mac that is offline from the start with its saved last-seen time. Wait ~20 s for the reconnect backoff before the screenshot.
5. Fixture runs skip the notification permission prompt (Debug builds). Erase the simulator if an old prompt is still queued.
