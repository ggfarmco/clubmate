B · Mac cards sheet

The capsule opens a sheet with one card per Mac: its colour, its state, its workspaces with status, and Mac Details as the card's own button. Quiet workspaces fold away; search when it gets long.

Glyph = status, the session row's 28pt circle: amber hand only for a live wait, red ✕ only for a live failure, grey play for running, grey folder idle, house for Home. The checkmark in accent marks the current one.

Fold rule: a card with more than 5 workspaces shows Home, the current one and those with activity, in the Mac's order; the rest sit behind "Show all N workspaces". Search replaces the fold.

Costs: a new screen to build, test and keep in step with the iPad sidebar; the fold rule moves rows as activity changes, so a row may not be where it was last time.
