A · Garage · pick
Your garage. Agents welcome. Set it up the way you like: your backlog, your wiki, your schedules, your processes.

The place is yours and comfortable; agents come in and work your way. "Welcome" is plural and open, so it names no vendor and leaves room for the next agent. Your words, almost: the garage is where you tinker, keep your tools and build your own setups.

B · Workshop
A workshop you set up yourself. Build your own processes, schedules and backlog; agents run them on every project and in Home.

Leads with building: graphs, gates, triggers. Strong for the processes story, but colder: it sounds like a tool to configure, and "workshop" was the r2 word the user moved past.

C · Clock in
Where your agents clock in. They pick up your backlog, keep your wiki, run on your schedules and follow the processes you built.

Tells the daily routine in one picture: cron, backlog, processes. Memorable, but "clock in" also reads as watching workers, and it says less about it being your place.
