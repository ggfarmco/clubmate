1 · Positioning · workshop

Pick (user, 23 Nov): B · "Your workshop, set up your way." The designer proposed A. r3's idea with the word the user likes: a place on your Mac that you set up your way, where agents of any make work with your backlog, your wiki, your schedules, your side threads and your processes.

A · Agents welcome
Your workshop. Agents welcome. Set it up your way: your backlog, wiki, schedules, side threads and processes.

A place you own, with the door open to any agent. Keeps everything r3 got right (warm, no vendor, room for the next agent) and swaps the room for the one you like. The second line is an invitation, so the agents are guests who work your way.

B · Set up your way · pick
Your workshop, set up your way. Agents work with your backlog, your wiki, your schedules and the processes you build.

Ownership first, and true to every part of the app. Warm, but the headline never says agents, so a stranger reads "a workspace app" until line three. Its words live on in A's sub-line and the About line.

C · Open all night
Your workshop, open all night. Agents work your backlog and your schedules while you're away; you come back to a brief.

The cron story in four words: you sleep, the workshop works. Charming, but it promises work around the clock, which needs the Mac awake, and it leaves out side threads and the wiki.

The hero, measured

  • One 64px margin on every side. Text column 64 → 349 (285 wide); gutter 48; screenshot 397 → 1216 × 64 → 576. Logo and screenshot both start at 64; the small print ends at 576 with the screenshot.
  • The screenshot is your aside shot as is (2560×1600), shown whole at 819×512 (0.64 of the 1280×800 window). No crop: it keeps the real title bar and status bar, so it reads as a window without a drawn frame. Only a 1px border and a shadow are added.
  • Why whole, not cropped: the four things worth proving sit in four corners: Needs you and the review loop top left, the Morning brief transcript bottom centre, the aside top and bottom right. Any crop that keeps text readable drops two of them.
  • Readability: at 0.64 the app's 12pt text is about 8px, and about 5px in the README at 880. The hero is read as a picture of a busy, real workshop, not as text; the README rows carry the readable detail at 1:1.
  • The headline wraps on purpose into four short lines (Your / workshop. / Agents / welcome.) so the window can be as large as the margins allow.
