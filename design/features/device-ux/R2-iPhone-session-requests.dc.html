A question card follows the “Question card fold” ruling (B · Peek): three heights, full · peek · bar, it opens at peek, and only you fold it to the bar.

Behaviour

  • Permission card: open on arrival. Folds when the live end is more than 120 pt below the visible bottom; opens within 24 pt again or on tap. The folded row never answers.
  • Question card: opens at peek (S3). Drag the grabber up for full (S4, ≤ 60% of the screen). Scrolling the transcript up drops full to peek, never to the bar. Only a tap on the header chevron, a grabber drag down or ⌥Q folds it to the bar (S5); the fold is kept for that question set.
  • Composer under a question is the current question's Other: chip “Other · for Retries…”, placeholder “Type your own answer…”. The plugin refuses a plain prompt, so there is no Queue field. ⌥1–⌥4 pick, ⌥Q fold, ⌘↩ send.
  • Amber count in the bar (other sessions waiting): a tap opens the oldest waiting one, whatever the count; Inbox lists them all.
  • Answered: the card becomes one muted line; if more wait, a Next row takes its place (S6).
