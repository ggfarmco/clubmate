A narrow pane: what changes

Only what the width forces: no sidebar, peek shows a window of lines instead of the range, the bar keeps the picked line, and the map pane becomes the header strip. Fold rules as decided: full in a tall window, peek in a short one, bar with ⌥Q. The map strip is used whenever the main column is under 960 px.
