Final · rules

Rules: states, fields, keys, folds, platforms

The plugin decides every state and sends it as a field; the UI only draws it. The Mac, iPhone and iPad show the same card. Components are listed in build order.

Open points · rulings

PointRuling, or the options
a · Keys: confidence vs optionsyour call

Boards draw option 1 until you decide.

  1. Options take a–d in guide sessions; digits 1–3 always rate. One rule on every guide card. Cost: the choice card has different keys in guide sessions than elsewhere. Recommended.
  2. Confidence on ⌥1–⌥3; options keep 1–4. Also works while typing. Cost: ⌥1–⌥3 are Allow / Always / Reject on the permission card, which can be docked at the same time; on a Mac ⌥ plus a digit types ¡ ™ £ in text fields.
  3. g / t / s. No clash with digits. Cost: dead while typing, and English-only mnemonics.
  4. Digits follow focus: options until one is picked, then confidence. No new keys. Cost: modal; the same key does two things a second apart.
b · Fixing a wrong pick or a mis-hit chipruledA pick never sends, so a wrong line is fixed by moving or picking again. A chip holds the answer for 2 s while a bar runs out under the card: ⌘Z or Undo goes back to the pick; another digit changes the rating and restarts the bar. Then the plugin grades it. There is no undo after the reveal, because the answer has been seen; the guide can ask again. your call only on the length: 2 s proposed, 0 means no hold.
c · Map: pane or stripruledThe map is the Tour tab of the right panel, next to Context; a guide session opens the panel on Tour. It shows as a pane when the main column (window minus rail and sessions list) is 960 px or wider and the panel is open, which keeps the transcript at about 690 px. Otherwise it is the header strip, and a click opens the same tree as a popover.
d · A Next step button?ruledNo permanent Next. The guide moves on by itself after every graded answer; the reveal is the pause. When a step has no check, the plugin adds a continue action, drawn as the only primary: Continue ⌘↩ (⌘↩ continues only while the composer is empty).
e · Range and text answers on the chipruledRange: yes. Chips stay off while ⇧ is held or a drag is under way and turn on once the range has both ends; then a chip sends. Text “why”: no. Chips only rate, ⌘↩ sends, and ⌘↩ without a rating moves focus to the chips. On the phone, a text answer shows the three ratings as a segmented control above Send.

4 and 5 on iPhone and iPad, no boards: controls are a row of capsules under the latest step (Continue first when the plugin sends it); the map opens from the step count under the title as a sheet on iPhone and as the header strip’s popover on iPad.

2 · Answer and reveal

Statescorrect · close · wrong · sure-and-wrong · checking (a typed answer waiting for the guide)
Fieldsverdict: correct | close | wrong · verdict_label · flag: sure_wrong · note · yours {lines, confidence} · answer {lines} · why · ref path:line · grading: instant | pending
Plugin decidesCorrect, close or wrong (what counts as close, e.g. an overlapping range), and sure-and-wrong. The UI draws a ✓ badge, a half-disc badge or a ✕ badge; sure-and-wrong adds a red confidence tag and the plugin’s one-line note.
PlacementIn place: the asking card becomes the record, marks on its lines, then verdict, one line of why (clamped to 2), the path. ⌘O opens the path in nvim.

3 · Confidence

Statesoff (nothing picked) · ready · sending (hold, ⌘Z) · set (text answers: rated, not sent) · stored (small tag in the record)
Fieldslevels ["guess", "think so", "sure"] · value · sends (true for line, range and choice; false for text) · hold_ms 2000 · required true
Plugin decidesWhether a rating is required and whether a chip sends; it stores the value with the answer. Sure-and-wrong is its call, not the UI’s.
Keys1–3 rate and send (pending ruling a) · ⌘Z undo during the hold · ⌘↩ sends a text answer

4 · Step controls

Statesunder the latest step only · hidden while a question is open · Go deeper pressed (aside open) · an action disabled (42%) · Continue present or not
Fieldsstep · actions: test_me, show_me, go_deeper, smaller_steps, continue, each {label, enabled} · pressed · aside {step, title, open}
Plugin decidesWhich actions show, which is enabled, whether Continue exists, whether an aside is open. Each button is a fixed request to the guide node.
Keys⌥T test me · ⌥S show me · ⌥D go deeper · ⌥M smaller steps · ⌘↩ continue (empty composer) · ⎋ leaves an aside

5 · Concept map with progress

Statesper concept: known · shaky · missed · current · not yet. Shown as: pane (Tour tab) · header strip · popover
Fieldsconcepts [{id, label, state, step}] · edges (from the run’s tour.json) · current · offers per concept: ask_again, go_back · counts
Plugin decidesEvery state and the current concept. For reference only, its rule today: correct + sure → known; correct + guess or think so → shaky; close → shaky; wrong → missed; sure-and-wrong → missed and asked again later.
Drawn asA tree in tour order; an edge to another branch is a ↗ chip. Filled ✓ · half disc · ring ✕ · ring with a dot and the row selected · dashed ring. A click opens only the offers the plugin sent.
Keys⌘⇧M shows or hides the panel (proposal; use the panel’s own key if it has one) · ↑↓ ↩ inside the tree

Fold behaviour (question card rules, decided 19 Nov)

FullMac: the whole range, in a tall window. iPad: capped at 60% of the column. iPhone: drag the peek up.
PeekMac: opens by itself when the full card would take more than ~45% of the pane; six lines around the cursor and “All N lines ⌥Q”. iPhone: the default, three lines around the pick plus the capsules. Scrolling the transcript drops full to peek, never further.
BarMac: ⌥Q. iPhone: drag the peek down. The bar keeps the pick (“line 32 · 1–3 rate, send”); on the phone a tap opens the peek to rate.
⌥QToggles full or peek ⇄ bar on Mac and on iPad with a keyboard.
ComposerAlways the question’s Other in every fold state: typed words are an answer the guide grades.
AfterAn answered card leaves the dock and stays in the transcript as a record; the dock is free again.
