What differs from the Mac

  • No keycaps. The action is a capsule: New session, or the New task… field + Add.
  • The Backlog state chips (Idea → … → Done) stay on the Mac; this round adds nothing to the phone.
  • Wiki sits under More, with the same node and the same words as the Mac (board 4).
  • The capsule reads “● Home ⌄”, and the dot is the Mac's identity colour. Nothing else tells the user they are in Home, and nothing else needs to.

What does not differ

  • Title, text and action come from the plugin, so they are written once for both clients.
  • No new SDUI node, no new prop.
