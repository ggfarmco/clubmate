Slot waits, trust, and where they show

The plugin decides every field: landing.words (the Mac row word, 9 characters at most), landing.line (segments for the phone and the headers), the status icon and the card's statusWord. Clients draw them. They never shorten a word or work out a state.

Settings caps an Id at 9 characters (a–z, 0–9, -). The plugin sends ‹id› slot when that fits in 9, otherwise ‹id› alone; the hourglass already says “waits”. Phone lines start with a capital, like Running and Editing. Mac words and the Mac header line stay lower case, like every other Mac row word.

Mac row: the word wins

  • Both options, together: the plugin sends a word of 9 characters or fewer, and the word keeps its full width. The title cuts.
  • RowFit's half-row rule for details goes. The word slot is never truncated: 9 characters is about 65 pt at 12 pt mono.
  • At a 248 pt sidebar the title gets 18 characters at depth 0 and 16 at depth 1, so “Companion UI tests” fits whole.
  • A word over 9 characters is a plugin bug. It's drawn whole anyway and the title gives way.
  • Tooltip: ‹title› · ‹full line›.
  • waits for trust leaves the Mac row. The row says trust with the shield; the phone and the header keep the sentence.

The line, in segments

  • landing.line is a list joined by · . The first segment is the state and never drops.
  • Place reads next in line or ‹n› ahead, not “2nd in line”. “2nd” counts the holder, but 1 of 1 in use counts holders on their own, so the two would disagree.
  • Mac header: the line after the title in the secondary ink, then Claude · main in the faint ink. Claude · main folds first, then segments drop from the end, then the title truncates.
  • iPad header: the whole line, then · ‹workspace›.
  • iPhone header: the first and last segments, then the workspace: Waits for test slot · 3 min · clubmate.
  • Time counts from when the wait began and ticks each minute.

Settings › Resource limits › Edit

Inline, fixed. A sheet would need presentation work in the Settings window for a form that edits one kind at a time, and inline keeps the live Now block next to the kind's own row.

  • Edit opens the kind's own row in place: Name, then the mono Id and its live summary. Add kind opens an empty one above the Add kind button. Only one is open at a time; Edit on another kind asks whether to keep or discard unsaved changes.
  • Card: window background inside the group, 1 pt hairline, radius 8, 14/16 pt inside, 12 pt between blocks. The note sits inside the card, never against its edge.
  • Fields: a 120 pt label column, leading-aligned, 12 pt to the control; 24 pt controls with 8 pt between rows. The Patterns label lines up with its first line.
  • Mono: Id, Patterns, and commands in Now. Commands truncate in the middle so the matched word (test) stays.
  • Now, under a hairline: summary, then Holding (title · command · time · Release) and Waiting (place · title · command · time). Release is on holders only. An empty list hides its heading; with both empty: Nothing in use.
  • Footer, under a hairline: Remove… leading and alone (it confirms); Reset to defaults · Cancel · Save trailing, 8 pt apart; Save is the default (⌘↩), Cancel answers esc.
  • Title: the kind's Name (Tests), never “Tests slots”.
