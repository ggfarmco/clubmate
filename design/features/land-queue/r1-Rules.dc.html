Landing queue in the Agents tree

One queue per repo and target branch. The plugin decides every field; the UI maps state + needsYou to a word, a glyph and a tone, and never works one out.

Needs you

Only the plugin's needsYou: today asking, and blocked by your own edits or a conflict nobody can fix. Then, on every device:

  • warning glyph and word on the entry row
  • the group detail turns to 1 needs you
  • it counts in the Needs you chip, the rail dot, the status bar and the bell
  • iPhone and iPad list it under Needs you too, subtitle Land into ‹branch›? or the why
  • opening it lands on the session's Land card
