3 · Settings → Keep Awake

Standard row page, two parts. Now is read-only, as in round 1: text, detail and the holds. Keep awake is one control: a radio group with Off and the three choices, the hours as a select inside their row. “On battery too” sits under While agents work, the only choice it affects, and only on laptops. “Keep the screen on” stays as its own row.

The pane

  • Header: the standard plugin header, unchanged.
  • Now: status line, detail, holds as a read-only property list. No buttons here: the radio group below is the control, and Off in it is Turn off.
  • Keep awake: four radios, one always selected. “For” carries a select (1 / 2 / 4 / 8 hours) and, while active, its end time in muted. Changing the number restarts the clock.
  • On battery too: indented under While agents work; you can set it before choosing. Laptops only.
  • Keep the screen on: own row under Display; applies to every hold.
  • Lid footnote last, muted, laptops only. Autosave.
