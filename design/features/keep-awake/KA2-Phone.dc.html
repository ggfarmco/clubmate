Answers for the phone

  • One picker, label “Keep awake”, value = Off · Until 18:00 · While agents work · Until turned off. That replaces round 1's two pickers and today's “Keep Awake…” and “Turn Off” accent rows.
  • Hours in a palette row rather than a nested submenu: on a phone a submenu is a second sheet, while a row of four 44pt buttons is one tap. The Mac uses a submenu because NSMenu palette items are images only (board 2).
  • While Agents Work gets a subtitle in the menu (“Running or waiting for you”), so the row needs no caption.
  • Status glyph: accent-tinted while awake (same as a running session), neutral when off or armed.
  • Footer: detail while awake; nothing while off or armed, because the status line already is detail.
  • Not on the phone: On battery too, Keep the screen on. They are Mac settings and rarely changed.
