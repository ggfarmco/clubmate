Anatomy

  1. Now, unchanged from round 1. The first line is text (or “The Mac sleeps as usual.”), with one quiet line per hold under it. Your choice is one line (“You · for 4 hours · until 18:00”, “While agents work · 2 sessions”); each agent's own hold is another. An agent's line opens its session; its hold is ended there, never from this menu.
  2. Keep Awake: the three choices, in order of commitment: for a while, while there's work, for good. ✓ marks the active one; picking another replaces it.
  3. For Hours ▸ opens 1 / 2 / 4 / 8. While active, its title says the number (“For 4 Hours”) and the submenu ✓s it. Picking another number restarts the clock from now.
  4. Turn Off only while a choice is active. It ends your choice; agents' own holds keep going, and the status line still shows them.
  5. ⌥⌘A sits on Until Turned Off when nothing is chosen and on Turn Off when something is: one key, the same toggle as ⌥-click on the cup.
  6. Keep Awake Settings… last. On battery too and Keep the screen on live there.

Wording

  • Header + item read as one phrase: “Keep Awake · For 4 Hours”, “Keep Awake · While Agents Work”, “Keep Awake · Until Turned Off”.
  • Title Case, as macOS menus are. The phone uses the same words (board 4).
  • Agent holds use the agent's own label and the session title: names over ids.
