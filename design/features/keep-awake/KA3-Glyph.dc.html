1 · The gourd glyph

The cup becomes the mate gourd with its bombilla, drawn like our other line icons: 16-unit grid, 1.4 stroke, round caps and joins, monochrome. The three looks are the same as round 2: off (outline, faint), armed (outline, muted), awake (the gourd filled, with a small yerba leaf at the mouth opposite the bombilla).

Construction

  • Body: shoulders narrowing into the mouth, not a circle. It is widest low (x 3.2–12.8 at y 10.4), sits on y 15 and is centred on x 8, so it reads as a calabash, not a pot.
  • Rim: one line at y 5.6, x 5–11: the icon's metal ring.
  • Bombilla: from the rim at x 9.3 up to (11.6, 1.3), about 28° off vertical, leaning right as in the icon.
  • Awake body: one filled shape, no stroke, drawn to the outline's outer edge (the stroked outline plus 0.7), so off and awake have the same size. Its top is cut flat at y 7.2, which leaves a 0.9 gap under the rim. That gap is what makes it a vessel and not a fruit.
  • Leaf (awake only): small, about 2.7 long, rising from the rim's left end at about 30° up and out, opposite the bombilla. A yerba sprig in the mouth.
  • The icon's “>_” is left off. At 14 pt it would be three pixels of noise.

Keeping the leaf mate, not apple

A leaf on top of a round shape with a stalk beside it is the apple silhouette. Two changes break it: the leaf moves down to the rim and leans out, and the body is cut below the rim, so the shape reads as a vessel with a mouth rather than a fruit.

Shipping it

  • SwiftUI: draw the paths as Shapes in a 16-unit space scaled to the frame. Stroke the rim, bombilla, leaf and outline body with StrokeStyle(lineWidth: 1.4 × scale, lineCap: .round, lineJoin: .round). Fill the awake body without a stroke; it's already drawn to the stroked edge. The leaf is filled and stroked.
  • SF Symbol: the template wants filled outlines, not strokes. Outline the strokes first (keep 1.4 at 16 = Regular), then place on the template's Regular-M guides and interpolate Ultralight/Black from there. Keep the 0.9 gap under the rim at every weight. Two symbols: clubmate.gourd (off and armed) and clubmate.gourd.fill (awake).
  • Sizes: 14 pt in the status bar, 14 in menus and Settings, 16 inside the phone's 28 pt status glyph, 13 in the Macs list. All from the same paths.
  • Same family as the monochrome gourd mark Principles allows for empty states: that mark can reuse the outline without the bombilla.
