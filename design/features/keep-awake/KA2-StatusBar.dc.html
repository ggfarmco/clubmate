Behaviour

  • Click or right-click: the menu (board 2). Item shows pressed (fg 11%) while it is open, hover (fg 6%) under the pointer. Same overlays as any ghost control.
  • ⌥-click: the Amphetamine quick toggle without the menu. With no choice active it picks Until Turned Off; with any choice active it turns it off. It is an accelerator only: both items are in the menu with ⌥⌘A printed next to them, and in the palette.
  • Palette: “Keep Awake for 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 Hours”, “Keep Awake While Agents Work”, “Keep Awake Until Turned Off”, “Turn Off Keep Awake”. Typing “awake” or “sleep” finds them.
  • Hold ends: the cup goes back to outline with no flash or toast. Nothing finished that needs you (Principle 3).
  • Accessibility: the item is a button labelled “Keep Awake” whose value is text or “Off”; the tooltip is its help text.
