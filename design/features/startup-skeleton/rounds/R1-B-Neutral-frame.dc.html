Clubmate · startup skeleton · round 1 · direction B

B · Neutral frame

No cache: only what the client knows today (section count from sectionOrder, sidebar side, theme, font size) and one generic shape in every pane.

For

  • Zero new state; nothing can be stale.
  • Simplest to build: one skeleton view.

Against

  • Layout jumps at ready whenever the sidebar was hidden, the chat column was shown or the sidebar was resized: the panes move under the fade.
  • Wrong shape in terminal panes: rows drawn where nvim or the shell will be.
  • The rail is anonymous: you can’t see where ⌘3 will take you.
  • 640: the same as A’s 640 with squares in the rail.
