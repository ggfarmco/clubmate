Rulings
  1. Q3 · order. Icon + title · sentence · primary New session N · hint. The action comes right after the sentence that explains it; the hint is a footnote.
  2. Q3 · look. The hint is muted caption text, not a chip: chips in this panel are context you can remove, and a chip that is neither clickable nor removable reads as one. It prints the key: “Closed sessions stay in Agents ⌘3”.
  3. Empty button. Primary, sized to its label (the empty node's rule), N as a boxed key cap. ⏎ also takes it: it is the only focusable thing.
  4. Header. The empty picker keeps the “Chat · pick a session” header with the hide button (F3); the shot has none.
