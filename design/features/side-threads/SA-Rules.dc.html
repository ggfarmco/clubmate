Side threads — decided 16 Nov: beside by default, its own page on request

An aside is a read-only fork of a session, opened from an option of a pending question or from a passage. It is an ordinary session page that opens beside its parent, so the card never leaves the screen; it expands to its own page with the question pinned on top. Send back through one preview, keep it as a session, or close it.

Decided 16 Nov

  1. The aside is an ordinary session page: its own page, with the parent's question pinned as a strip on top, so you can pick from there (from C).
  2. By default it opens beside the parent: a split at 1280, stacked under the parent at 640 (from A). From there it expands to its full page and goes back beside: Full page / Beside ⌘⇧\.
  3. Phone: a sheet over the session by default; its expand button opens the ordinary pushed session screen with the question strip.
  4. Close: ⌥⌘⌫, the same key as Close Session, plus the × in the aside's header. It asks once, only when something is unsent. ⌘W keeps its meanings and does not close an aside.
  5. Keep: the aside leaves its parent for the top level of the tree (its worktree group), with “forked from ‹parent›” in its header.
  6. Send back: “close the aside after” is on by default.
  7. Unchanged from A: a on an option, ⌘\ on a selection, ⌘[ / ⌘], ⌘⇧↩ Send back (Answer question / Draft in its composer · Summary / Last message / Quote), ⌘⇧K Keep, the states and Needs you.

Questions 1–6 in the final direction

1 · Where it opens

Beside the parent by default: a split in the Agents main pane at 1280 (parent left, aside right, the edge drags), stacked under the parent at 640. Full page on request: the aside's own session page with the question strip (above). Phone: a sheet over the session, the card visible above it; swipe down to the card, its Next / Send work as before. ⤢ in the sheet's header pushes the ordinary session screen.

2 · From an option

One key on the focused option: a — Ask aside, next to the card's 1–4 and o. Only the row with focus or the pointer shows “Ask aside a”, so a 4 × 4 card stays as it is. The option's right-click menu has Ask aside too. A row that has an aside shows a small “aside” mark; a on it reopens that aside. Phone: long-press an option → Ask aside.

3 · From a selection

Mac: Ask aside ⌘\ is the first item of the prose text view's menu, above Copy and Look Up; ⌘\ with a selection does the same, with nothing selected it asks about the whole last answer. The passage keeps a tint, a dashed underline and an aside mark in the margin. Phone: long-press a message → Ask aside; the whole message is the passage, and the quote inside the aside is selectable to narrow it.

4 · How it is marked

In the tree an aside hangs under its parent with the aside glyph and the label “Aside · ‹option›” or “Aside · “‹passage…›””; a child an agent started keeps the agent glyph and its own title. Its header says read-only and where it came from. In the parent, the option row shows “aside” and the passage keeps its mark; clicking either, or ⌘], focuses the aside beside. ⌘[ in the aside goes to the parent and scrolls to the anchor.

5 · Send back

A preview panel at the end of the aside (⌘⇧↩); nothing leaves without it. To: “Answer question 1” (an aside from an option): picks that option in the parent's card and adds an editable note; the card still waits for your Send. Or “Draft in its composer” (default for a passage). From: a Summary Claude writes on request (default), the Last message, or a Quote you pick. The text is editable; “close the aside after” is on. ⌘↩ sends, ⎋ cancels.

6 · Keep and close

In the aside's header: Send back ⌘⇧↩ (primary), Keep ⌘⇧K, Full page / Beside ⌘⇧\, and × ⌥⌘⌫ at the far end. Keep: the aside leaves its parent for the top level of its worktree group, the aside glyph becomes the session glyph, “read-only” goes, the mode switches from Plan to the parent's mode, the header keeps “forked from ‹parent›”, and it stays where it is on screen. Close (⌥⌘⌫ or ×) deletes it; only when something is unsent it asks once: “Close without sending? · Send back… · Close”.

7 · Several asides

One aside per anchor: asking again about the same option or passage reopens it. In the tree, asides hang under their parent after the children an agent started, newest first; from three on they fold into one row “Asides · 3”. Beside shows one aside at a time; its header gets “‹ 2 of 3 ›” and ⌘] steps through them. The card marks each option that has one. On the phone the parent's title bar gets a pill “2 asides” that lists them in a sheet.

8 · Needs you

An open aside doesn't count, unless it asks you itself. The plugin owns the state and the label; Mac and phone draw them.

Handoff

aAgents main pane: the aside beside the parent — a split at 1280, stacked at 640, draggable edge, two transcripts — and the switch to the aside's own page and back (⌘⇧\, ⤢ / ⤡ in the headers). Beside or full is a view choice the client keeps per aside.
aProse text view: “Ask aside ⌘\” as the first menu item; the anchor tint, dashed underline and margin glyph, clickable. Phone: “Ask aside” in the message long-press menu; a selectable quote inside the aside.
aPhone: the aside sheet (one detent, card visible above) with ⤢ and ×; ⤢ pushes the ordinary session screen with ⤡ in its bar; ‹ and ⤡ return to the sheet. The question strip there is drawn natively.
aAside glyph, expand / collapse glyphs, the read-only pill. Keys: a, ⌘\, ⌘⇧\, ⌘], ⌘⇧↩, ⌘⇧K; ⌥⌘⌫ routed to Close in an aside.
bchoice option prop aside {id, state}: the “aside” mark, the “Ask aside a” hint on focus, the a key.
bQuestion card answer prop note: a per-question note under the options, prefilled by Send back, editable (carried as the AskUserQuestion annotation note).
bQuestion card prop pinned: one question drawn as a strip — header chip, question, options as chips, the aside's option marked; picking writes to the parent's card. The plugin sends it only on the aside's full page.
bSend back panel from existing nodes (card, select, textField, toggle, button): no new node.
cWords: “Ask aside”, “Aside · ‹option›”, “read-only”, “Full page” / “Beside”, “‹parent› asks”, “Pick here: it answers question 1 in the parent's card…”, “Forked from the question · read everything the session knew”, “forked from ‹parent›”, “Send back”, “Answer question 1” / “Draft in its composer”, “Summary · Last message · Quote…”, “close the aside after” (on), “Keep”, “Close without sending?”.
cOrder: asides after agent-started children under a parent, newest first; fold into “Asides · N” from 3. A kept aside goes to the top level of its worktree group.
dAside session kind: fork of the parent at an anchor — {question id, option index} or {message id, range}; plan mode; parentId; one per anchor.
dCommands: askAside(anchor), summarize (on request), sendBack{to: answer|draft, from: summary|last|quote, text, close = true}, keep (lifts plan mode, takes the parent's mode, reparents to the top level, keeps forkedFrom), close (the Close Session command on an aside: delete, reports unsent first).
dStrip answers: a pick in the pinned strip sets the parent's pending answer for that question, the same write as the card; the card and the strip stay in sync.
dStates and Needs you as in the table: an aside counts only when it asks itself; the parent's state is untouched by its asides.
