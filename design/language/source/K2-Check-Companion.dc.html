Decided

  • Code chat on a graph step: not chosen by default; if picked by hand, it explains the step, offers Open in Graphs / New chat, and the composer stays off until the loop has run it.
  • Always allow on the Mac: ⌥2 opens a confirm with the rule and scope; Cancel is the default, ⌥2 again confirms. Labels on both clients: Allow once · Always allow… · Reject.
  • Offline Mac: its waiting items stay in Needs you, dimmed and read-only, with “as of 13:43 — you can answer when it's back”.
