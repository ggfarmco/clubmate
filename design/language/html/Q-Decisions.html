Open decisions and offers

About a quarter of turn endings hide a real decision in the report and two fifths end with an offer. A decision that is only text is a decision the user never sees. Three directions; the recommendation is 2 — done, with open items, keeping today's card (fixed) for questions that block.

Recommendation: 2 (B)

  • Two meanings, two looks. A blocking question is the amber card and the waiting state, as today. An end-of-turn card is neutral, starts with ✓ and says “3 open”: the work is done, nothing waits.
  • It stays where the context is. The card sits under the report; the report sentence carries a numbered ref, and every item quotes one line of it, so an item can be answered without reading the report. Every option has a one-line description.
  • One pass when there are many. The Open filter turns Agents into a queue across sessions (⌥O), and the phone has “Answer all”. Answers are sent per session.
  • Needs you stays honest. Open items never count as Needs you; they have their own quiet count.
  • Mixed card. Decisions are numbered single-choice items; offers are one “Next — tick what you want me to do” item with checkboxes. Send answers starts one turn with all of it.
  • AFK endings post the same record instead of listing questions in prose.

6 · Skip and later

Skip goes away on end-of-turn cards. Instead:

  • Later (L) — keep them open here and in Open; no reminders. Ignoring the card is the same as Later.
  • To backlog (B) — one task per item, with the quote and a link to the session. Per item or all.
  • No to all (⌫ for one, ⇧⌫ for all, in •••) — closes them; Claude is told “none of these” next turn.
  • After 7 days, or when the session is closed, open items move to the backlog by themselves.

Blocking cards keep Skip, meaning “none of these, go on”.

7 · Limits

  • The record is ours, so the 4-question cap doesn't apply. A card shows at most 3 decisions + 4 offers (7 items); the agent files anything beyond that in the backlog, and the card shows “+2 more went to the backlog”.
  • Each decision keeps AskUserQuestion's shape: 2–4 options, header ≤ 12 chars, Other.
  • Blocking (A) cards keep the 4-question cap; the agent asks again after the answer.

Today's card, fixed in every direction

  • Buried decisions read done. Direction 2 gives them a state; direction 1 makes them waiting. Both need the agent to post them (c), with the optional safety net (d).
  • The “—” popup. Single choice is always radio rows with descriptions; one “Other…” row per question instead of an extra field (a).
  • The 300 pt cap on the phone. One question per page with a stepper (a).

Handoff

aMac question card: a single-choice question always renders as radio rows with descriptions — no “—” popup, even with 2+ questions. Multi-select keeps its descriptions. One “Other…” row per question opens a field only when picked.
aPhone question card: no 300 pt cap. With 2+ questions, one question per page with a 1-of-N stepper (Back / Next, Skip on page 1).
aNarrow Mac card (≤ 800 pt): the focused item is expanded and the others fold to one line with their answer (accordion).
aThe outlined amber count (“3 open”) and the numbered ref chip (①) as renderer styles; colours are roles (warning ink, border) from the Ghostty palette.
bchoice rows: radio or check, label, description, key hint, on — replacing the popup and the toggles.
bqitem: number, header chip (≤ 12 chars), question, one context line, choices, fold state with the answer.
bref inline in a text node: a numbered chip that scrolls to and focuses item n.
bopenCount on the session row, the session header and the status bar.
cAgent instruction (and AFK): never end a turn with a decision or an offer in prose; post them as open items. At most 3 decisions + 4 offers; every option has a one-line description; each item carries one line of context from the report.
cWords: “Open after this turn”, “3 open”, “Send answers”, “Later”, “To backlog”, “No to all”; for blocking cards “Claude is asking” stays.
cThe agent's answer turn starts with what it got: “Answers: Lists → SDUI prop; Grouping → own group; do: ⌘/ screen”.
dAgent tool open_items (or AskUserQuestion with final: true): items in AskUserQuestion's shape (header, options with label + description, multiSelect, Other) plus quote (the report sentence) and kind decision | offer. The turn ends done.
dNew session state open in needsYou: not in Needs you, counted as Open; labels as in the table. Answering starts a turn; all answered or closed → done.
dOpen queue across sessions (Mac: the Open filter in Agents, ⌥O; phone: Answer all). Answers are sent per session, batched.
dLater (keeps open, silences reminders), To backlog (one task per item, with quote + link), No (tells the agent “none of these”). After 7 days, or when the session is closed, open items move to the backlog by themselves, with a line in the session.
dClosed or gone session: answers start a new session in its worktree (offers) or go to the backlog (decisions), as the user picks in the queue.
dChild sessions: a child's open items go to its parent's record, never to the user directly; the parent answers or carries them into its own end of turn.
dSafety net (optional): if a turn ends with no record but the last paragraph matches “your call / should it… / if you want, I can”, the row shows a faint “may have left a question” and Open offers “Turn into items”.
