3 · Tokens

Ghostty gives us bg, fg and four tones. Theme.swift computes three numbers per theme (text ratios that pass contrast, and tone inks); everything else is a fixed ratio. Sizes are multiples of u = uiSize (12pt by default), rounded to whole points.
