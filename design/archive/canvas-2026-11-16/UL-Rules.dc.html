Decided 16 Nov

  1. A, in place. The account's two windows are one caption line in the workspace header; the session's 5-hour share joins its subtitle from 1%; ••• → Session info holds the rest.
  2. Close to a limit, the line changes: amber text on warning and danger text when reached, the same on Mac and phone. Sessions the limit stopped gather under Paused until ‹reset› after Needs you, on phone and Mac. No banner, no notification, nothing in Needs you.
  3. Accounts: paired Macs can use different Claude accounts. The plugin sends an account id with the windows; the phone keeps each account's numbers apart and never adds them up.
  4. The Mac keeps its header text and status bar; the iPad carries the line in its sidebar footer.

Question by question

1 · Account limits

In the workspace header, right-aligned on the Mac line: 5h 42% · 7d 81%, caption ink with the numbers in secondary ink, no reset times while both are allowed. The line is per workspace: it shows the account of that workspace's Mac. Tapping it opens Claude usage: this Mac's account first, then any other account your Macs use, each with both windows, resets and “via ‹Mac› · 2 min ago”. A window not reported yet is left out; a window whose reset time has passed is hidden until the next report.

2 · A session's share

In the session subtitle: “Waiting for you · shop · ≈6% of 5h” — the 5-hour share only, from 1%. ••• → Session info: context, the share of both windows, one sentence on the estimate, and the Claude account group, which names the account (its email) above its two windows. List rows show no share; the ≈ marks every share as an estimate.

3 · Close to a limit

Warning: the line narrows to the window in trouble and adds its reset — “5h 91% · resets 19:03” in amber text. Reached: “5h limit reached · resets 19:03” in danger text; stopped sessions move into Paused until 19:03 after Needs you, each still reading “Stopped by the usage limit · resumes 19:03”. No banner, no notification, nothing in Needs you: a limit can't be answered. The 7-day window works the same (“7d limit reached · resets Wed”).

4 · The Mac

The header's ≈6% of 5h · ≈3% of 7d and the status bar stay, warning in amber text as today. The Mac takes the same plugin words and the same “Paused until ‹reset›” group in its tree.

5 · iPad

The same as the phone, plus the account line all the time in the sidebar footer (so the workspace header doesn't repeat it) and both shares in the session header's meta line, as on the Mac.

Handoff

aPhone workspace header: the usage text right-aligned on the Mac line, ink by status, tappable to Claude usage.
aAccount label: shown only when the phone knows more than one account id; the email up to “@”, plus the domain's first part when two accounts share that part.
aPhone session subtitle: append the 5-hour share label when it is 1% or more.
aPhone sheets: Session info behind ••• (“This session”, the estimate sentence, “Claude account” with the email row) and Claude usage from the header line (this Mac's account, then your other Macs').
aRender rules: per account id, the newest updatedAt wins; leave out a window not yet reported; hide a window once its resetsAt has passed; never sum across accounts.
aiPad: the account line in the sidebar footer; both shares in the session header's meta line.
cWords, shared by Mac and phone: “5h 42% · 7d 81%”, “5h 91% · resets 19:03”, “5h limit reached · resets 19:03”, “≈6% of 5h”, “<1%”, “Share of 5-hour window”, “Claude account”, “Claude usage”, “This Mac's account”, “Your other Macs”, “via ‹Mac› · 2 min ago”, the estimate sentence, “Paused until ‹reset›”.
cGrouping: sessions stopped by a usage limit go under “Paused until ‹reset›” after Needs you, in the phone list and the Mac tree; they never count toward Needs you.
dCompanion feed: per Mac, the account {accountId, email} and its windows {used, resetsAt, status, updatedAt}; per session {share5h, share7d}. Today these reach only the Mac; the account id is new.
